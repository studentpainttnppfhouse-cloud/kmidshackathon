# Product

## Register

product

## Users

All KMIDS Hackathon staff, including members, department heads, administrators and advisors. Staff check work on phones between activities and coordinate teams on laptops.

## Product Purpose

One shared workspace for assignments, department teams, documents, files and staff updates. Help every staff member find their next task and the people and resources needed to complete it.

## Brand Personality

Clear, friendly, dependable. Keep the established pink Hackathon Studio identity.

## Anti-references

Flat menus that give every tool equal priority, decorative dashboard heroes, hidden daily actions on phones, and management screens that overwhelm ordinary staff.

## Design Principles

- Put daily work first for every staff member.
- Organize people and shared work around department teams, inspired by Microsoft Teams.
- Keep frequent destinations directly available on phones and laptops.
- Use plain labels and consistent controls.
- Preserve server-enforced permissions and existing workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain the existing accessible theme tokens, keyboard focus, reduced-motion support and light/dark themes. Use readable text, generous touch targets, semantic navigation and native dialog focus management.
